# Sale-KPI-Dashboard_Excel
## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales KPI Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze business performance across revenue, profit, COGS, customers, products, cities, and sales representatives.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.
## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor key sales and financial KPIs
- Analyze revenue, profit, and COGS
- Identify high- and low-performing products
- Evaluate sales representative performance
- Analyze customer trends
- Compare performance across cities
- Provide actionable business insights
## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Power Pivot
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Laptop AP13 generated the highest product profit at approximately $105M.
- Peter recorded the highest revenue among the sales representatives at approximately $435M.
- Lagos and Kano recorded the highest COGS among the cities analyzed.
- The business generated approximately $465.67M in profit, with a profit margin of about 20%.
- Customer numbers increased from January to April, indicating positive customer growth.
- The May customer figure should be validated before making business decisions.
- ## 📌 Business Recommendations
- Investigate the high COGS recorded in Lagos and Kano.
- Analyze what is driving the strong profitability of Laptop AP13.
- Review low-profit products for possible pricing or cost optimization.
- Identify the strategies used by top-performing sales representatives.
- Validate the May customer data before drawing conclusions.
